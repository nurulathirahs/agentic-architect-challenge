TECHNICAL FAQ

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login screen, enter your 
   registered email, and follow the link sent to your inbox to 
   reset your password.

Q: What are the system requirements?
A: Our platform supports modern browsers (Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+
   , Safari 14+, Edge 90+) on Windows 10/11, macOS 11+, Linux, 
   iOS, and Android.

Q: How do I resolve Error Code 500 or Connection Timeout?
A: Clear your browser cache and cookies, disable conflicting 
   browser extensions, and verify your network connection. If 
   issue persists, check status.example.com for service 
   maintenance updates.

Q: Can I integrate using API keys?
A: Yes, API keys can be generated under Developer Settings > API
   Keys. See our documentation for full REST API specs.
