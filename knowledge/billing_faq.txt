BILLING FAQ

Q: How do I change my credit card details?
A: Log in to your Account Dashboard, navigate to Settings 
   > Billing, and click "Update Payment Method".

Q: Where can I download my invoices?
A: Go to Settings > Billing > Invoice History. All past receipts
   are available for download as PDF documents.

Q: What billing cycles do you offer?
A: We offer Monthly and Annual subscriptions.Annual subscriptions
   come with a 20% discount.

Q: What happens if a payment fails?
A: Our system will retry the charge 3 times over a 7-day grace 
   period. If unpaid after 7 days, subscription features will 
   be temporarily paused.
