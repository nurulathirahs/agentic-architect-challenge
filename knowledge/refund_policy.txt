REFUND POLICY

1. Overview:
Customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days 
of purchasing any software subscription or digital product.

2. Refund Process:
To request a refund, submit an email with your Order ID 
and billing email address. Refunds are processed within 3 to 
5 business days back to the original payment method.

3. Exceptions & Non-Refundable Items:
- Custom enterprise development services are non-refundable after
 work has commenced.
- Refund requests submitted after 30 days are subject to store 
credit or partial refund depending on usage.

4. Contact Support:
For expedited processing, include "Refund Request" in your 
subject line along with your transaction receipt.
